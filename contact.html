FAQ

How can I join The Data Street?

Fill out the contact form above or email us at info@thedatastreet.com. We'd love to have you join our community!

What activities do you organize?

We organize workshops, seminars, hackathons, networking events, and collaborative projects throughout the year.

Is there a membership fee?

Most activities are free for students. Some specialized workshops may have a small fee to cover materials.

Can I volunteer or contribute?

We welcome volunteers and contributors. Reach out via email to discuss how you can get involved.
